## 🧠 How I Approach Data Problems

I try to follow this thought process:

```text
Business Problem
       ↓
Define the Metric
       ↓
Understand the Data
       ↓
Validate the Data
       ↓
Find the Trend
       ↓
Break the Metric into Drivers
       ↓
Test Possible Causes
       ↓
Find Supporting Evidence
       ↓
Business Recommendation
```

For example:

```text
Revenue
   ↓
Orders × AOV
   ↓
Orders = Customers × Purchase Frequency
   ↓
AOV = Units per Order × ASP
   ↓
Investigate the driver that changed
```

This helps me avoid jumping directly from a number to a conclusion.
